## Semantic validation v1

The strengthened `research/model_validation/validate.py` checks every declared site at batch sizes 1 and 2 for EEGPT, both BIOT checkpoints, and BENDR encoder: 68 site/batch conditions across three families. It compares removal of the first two feature coordinates, both zero-centered and nonzero-centered, against independent native-axis slice arithmetic. Outputs must match exactly, untouched feature coordinates must match exactly, and sensor/patch selectors must raise the expected error and clean up hooks. Retained whole-zero/identity/reordered-donor checks remain complementary.

Native feature axes are last for EEGPT (including its folded windows) and BIOT, and axis 1 for the BENDR convolutional encoder. Centered native arithmetic uses subtraction rather than assignment to preserve floating-point cancellation order. No tolerance was relaxed to accept downstream differences. These coordinate-subspace checks do not yet validate arbitrary dense bases or biological concepts.

Evidence: `research/model_validation/results/semantic-v1/{eegpt,biot,biot-prest,bendr}.json`. Records are separate from historical baseline validations. No new result is counted toward all eleven until its exact adapter/view has equivalent evidence. Evidence: `bendr-context.json`.
